# IDews: Intelligent Dam Early Warning System
**Team:** Nabd Qatra (نبض القطرة)  
**Event:** Arab Youth Space Hackathon / NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026  

---

## 📌 Overview
**IDews** is an advanced, space-driven early warning system designed to monitor dam structural integrity and water levels. By integrating Very High Resolution (VHR) satellite data (Sat-813 focus), hydro-meteorological precipitation data, and AI-driven predictive modeling, IDews delivers a **14-day predictive lead window** to prevent catastrophic flash floods and enable proactive, safe reservoir management.

---

## 🎯 The Problem & Human Catalyst
On January 29, 2026, at 02:14 AM, the Ksar El Kebir disaster struck near the Oued El Makhazen dam due to reactive, late, and uncoordinated water releases. Traditional disaster management suffers from **"temporal blindness,"** waiting for crises to unfold rather than predicting them. IDews solves this by providing automated, proactive alerts directly to competent authorities.

---

## 🛰️ Technical Architecture & Workflow
1. **Orbital Ingestion:** Continuous monitoring of dam surface water extent and basin topography using satellite imagery and SAR data.
2. **AI & Math Engine:** Deep learning and regression models built in Python to process hydro-meteorological data and forecast inflow volumes.
3. **Automated Alert Pipeline:** Tiered risk assessment generating automated, precise instructions for authorities to execute controlled, early water releases.
